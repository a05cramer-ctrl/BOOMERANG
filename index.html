How it comes back

Your buy pays a fee. Half of it belongs to the creator — and the creator wallet is the BOOMERANG engine, which throws it straight back to you.

  1. You

    Buy $BOOMERANG

    On StonkFun, paired with NVDAx.

  2. 1

    1%

    Pool fee, paid in NVDAx

    Every $BOOMERANG trade pays StonkFun's 1% pool fee in NVDAx (tokenized NVIDIA stock).

  3. 2

    ~0.5%

    StonkFun forwards the creator's half

    StonkFun keeps its half and forwards the creator's half (~0.5%) to the creator wallet every few minutes.

  4. 3

    15 s

    The engine throws it back

    The BOOMERANG engine is that wallet. 15 seconds after your buy, if you still hold, you get the creator half of the fee your buy paid (~0.5% of your buy) in NVDAx.

Back to you 15 s after your buyup to 1% of the buy when sellers charged the pot

  • Paper hands drop it

    Sell within 15 s and your boomerang drops into the pot.

  • Sellers charge the pot

    Sell-side fees charge a pot that tops a boomerang up to 1% of the buy.

  • No NVDAx account yet?

    Boomerangs stack until worth $1, then land in a new account the engine pays for.

The dev keeps 0% of the creator fee.
